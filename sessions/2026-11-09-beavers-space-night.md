# Beavers Space Night

Inspired by Scouts' Tim Peake *Peake Performance* activity. Four groups of approximately 5–7 Beavers rotate around four leader-led stations.

## Objectives
- Explore astronauts' fitness, teamwork and problem-solving.
- Identify features of Earth and order the eight planets.
- Design a rocket and explain its purpose.

## Consolidated equipment
- LEGO/blocks or scrap paper and cardboard, tape, pencils, optional scissors.
- 4–6 cones or bottles, floor tape or string, optional timer.
- Eight scraps with planet names; optional ball.
- Kitchen tongs or litter picker, six rolled socks/beanbags, two boxes.
- Timer or whistle; four leader copies of station instructions.

Assign one leader per station. Give a two-minute warning near the end of each rotation. Rotate groups, not equipment.

## Opening script
“Tonight we're going into space! British astronaut Tim Peake lived and worked on the International Space Station, orbiting Earth. Astronauts need to build things, stay fit, understand our planet and work together. You're going to train as space explorers at four mission stations. Stay with your team, listen to the leader at each station and have fun. Ready for launch?”

## Closing script
“Mission complete! What did astronauts need tonight? Fitness, teamwork, knowledge and good ideas. Who can tell me one thing that makes Earth special? Well done, space explorers!”
