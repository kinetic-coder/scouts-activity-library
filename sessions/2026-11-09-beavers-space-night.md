# Beavers Space Night

Four groups of up to six Beavers rotate around the latest four leader-led activities: Solar System Planet Order Challenge, Robot Arm Relay Challenge, Design Your Own Space Rocket and Space Station Charades. The local 18:30–19:45 session is 75 minutes; this timetable totals 75 minutes, including opening, three transitions, a separate rocket show-and-tell, tidy-up, closing and collection.

## Objectives
- Identify the Sun as a star, order the eight planets and recognise Pluto as a dwarf planet.
- Explain one way astronauts complete a daily task differently on the ISS.
- Design and label a rocket, explaining one feature that supports its mission.
- Demonstrate careful teamwork while using a grabber tool.

### Station rotation

Assign the four groups to different starting stations and rotate in the same direction after each two-minute movement. Keep groups together and rotate groups, not equipment. If fewer than four groups attend, leaders should combine or pause a station rather than making individual Beavers rotate alone.

1. [Solar System Planet Order Challenge](../activities/earth-solar-system-mission.md)
2. [Robot Arm Relay Challenge](../activities/robotic-arm-mission-control.md)
3. [Design Your Own Space Rocket](../activities/rocket-building.md)
4. [Space Station Charades: Life as an Astronaut](../activities/space-station-charades.md)

## Opening script
“Tonight we're going into space. You'll work in a mission team; choose one name: Apollo, Artemis, Galileo, Rosetta, Voyager or Ariane. These are names from real space missions, people or launch vehicles — they are not all astronaut names. Stay with your team, listen to your station leader and have fun. Ready for launch?”

## Closing script
“Mission complete! What did astronauts need tonight? Fitness, teamwork, knowledge and good ideas. Who can tell me one thing that makes Earth special? Well done, space explorers!”
