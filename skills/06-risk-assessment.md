# Skill: Risk assessment
List hazards, who may be harmed, initial risk (likelihood x severity using the group's agreed matrix), controls, residual risk, owner and further actions. Include assessor, assessment date, review date and whether a separate specialist assessment is required. Refer to existing group policies instead of copying them. Consider environment, equipment, safeguarding, accessibility and emergencies. Do not invent a risk score when the group's matrix is unknown; mark `TBC`. The responsible leader must review and approve before the activity, including changed conditions. This is planning support, not automatic safety approval.
