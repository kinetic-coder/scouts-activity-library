🦸 Marvel Quiz: Leader Answer Sheet

  1. What is Spider-Man's real name?
    Answer: Peter Parker
    Peter Parker is Spider-Man.
  2. Which superhero carries a shield with a star?
    Answer: Captain America
    Captain America carries the famous shield.
  3. What is Thor's hammer called?
    Answer: Mjolnir
    Thor's enchanted hammer is Mjolnir.
  4. True or false: Hulk is usually green.
    Answer: True
    The best-known version of Hulk is green.
  5. Which hero wears a high-tech suit of armour?
    Answer: Iron Man
    Tony Stark wears Iron Man armour.
  6. What is the name of Black Panther's home country?
    Answer: Wakanda
    Black Panther protects Wakanda.
  7. Which superhero can shrink to the size of an insect?
    Answer: Ant-Man
    Ant-Man can shrink using Pym Particles.
  8. True or false: Groot often says 'I am Groot'.
    Answer: True
    Groot's signature phrase is 'I am Groot'.
  9. Which Marvel team includes Star-Lord and Rocket?
    Answer: Guardians of the Galaxy
    Star-Lord and Rocket are Guardians of the Galaxy.
  10. Which Infinity Stone is in the centre of Vision's forehead in the MCU?
    Answer: Mind Stone
    Vision has the Mind Stone in his forehead.
