🚀 Cubs Space Quiz: Leader Answers

One point per correct answer; maximum 10. Read the explanation after each reveal.

  1. How many planets are in our Solar System?
    B — Eight
    Pluto is classed as a dwarf planet.
  2. Which is the largest planet?
    C — Jupiter
    Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System.
  3. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
    A — Mars
    Iron minerals in Martian soil oxidise, or rust, making Mars look red.
  4. What kind of object is the Sun?
    B — A star
    The Sun is the one star at the centre of our Solar System.
  5. Which planet is the hottest?
    B — Venus
    Venus is hottest because its thick atmosphere traps heat.
  6. Which mission first landed people on the Moon?
    A — Apollo 11
    Apollo 11 first landed people on the Moon in 1969.
  7. About how strong is gravity on the Moon compared with Earth?
    B — One sixth
    The Moon's surface gravity is about one sixth of Earth's.
  8. Which planet is famous for its bright rings?
    A — Saturn
    All four giant planets have rings, but Saturn's are the brightest and most extensive.
  9. True or false: a day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus?
    A — True
    Venus rotates so slowly that one full spin takes longer than one orbit around the Sun.
  10. Why do astronauts float inside the International Space Station?
    B — They and the station are falling around Earth together
    This continuous free fall creates microgravity; gravity has not disappeared.
