🎵 Cubs Music Quiz: Leader Answer Sheet

  1. How many strings does a guitar usually have?
    Answer: Six
    Most standard guitars have six strings.
  2. True or false: a piano has strings inside it?
    Answer: True
    Hammers strike strings inside an acoustic piano.
  3. Which musical word means fast and lively?
    Answer: Allegro
    Allegro means fast and lively.
  4. What do we call a group of people singing together?
    Answer: Choir
    A choir sings together.
  5. True or false: drums are percussion instruments.
    Answer: True
    Drums are percussion instruments.
  6. Which instrument do you blow into?
    Answer: Trumpet
    A trumpet is played by blowing through a mouthpiece.
  7. How many beats are in a bar of 4/4 time?
    Answer: Four
    There are four beats in a bar of 4/4.
  8. Which musical word means quiet or soft?
    Answer: Piano
    Piano means soft.
  9. True or false: sound travels faster through air than water.
    Answer: False
    Sound generally travels faster through water.
  10. Which of these is a woodwind instrument?
    Answer: Flute
    A flute belongs to the woodwind family.
