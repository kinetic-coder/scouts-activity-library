# Earth and Solar System Mission

## Purpose
Explore space-related skills through a short cooperative activity. Suitable for ages 6–8 with adaptations.

## Equipment
Eight planet-name scraps; optional ball; paper and pencils.

## Setup and instructions
1. Set up a supervised station before the meeting.
2. Discuss Earth's water, air and suitable temperature, and its appearance from space. Put Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune in order. Ask whether the Sun is a planet (no, star) and the Moon is a planet (no, satellite). Share one fact each.
3. Tidy and reset for the next group.

## Accessibility and adaptations
Offer paired help, verbal instructions and participation without physical exertion where appropriate.

## Risk controls
Avoid rushing or excluding children who need reading support; offer spoken clues and verbal ordering. Leader to review on the day and follow group risk assessment.
