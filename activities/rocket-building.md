# Rocket Building

## Purpose
Explore space-related skills through a short cooperative activity. Suitable for ages 6–8 with adaptations.

## Equipment
LEGO or blocks, or scrap paper/cardboard; tape; pencils; optional adult-supervised scissors.

## Setup and instructions
1. Set up a supervised station before the meeting.
2. Explain that rockets carry spacecraft and supplies. In pairs or small teams build or draw a rocket, name it, discuss where crew sit, launch and payload. Each team gives a 20-second show-and-tell. Reset materials.
3. Tidy and reset for the next group.

## Accessibility and adaptations
Offer paired help, verbal instructions and participation without physical exertion where appropriate.

## Risk controls
Scissors only with adult supervision; keep small parts away from mouths. Provide drawing alternative. Leader to review on the day and follow group risk assessment.
