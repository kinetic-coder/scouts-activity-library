# Astronaut Fitness Circuit

## Purpose
Explore space-related skills through a short cooperative activity. Suitable for ages 6–8 with adaptations.

## Equipment
4–6 cones, masking tape; timer on phone, A4 paper and pen / pencil to record results.

## Setup
1. Allocate a leader to setup and supervise the work area.
2. Setup the following course... Zig zag line, table to go under (mind head), skipping rope and star jumps. 

## Instructions
1. Explain to the young people that astronauts exercise to keep muscles and bones strong.  The no gravity environment means that our bodies do not get the same amount of exercise when walking and excercising on Earth due to gravity whilst floating around in a no gravity environment in space. 
2. Get each team member to do the course and time each person. Course will be...
  a. Walk the zig zag
  b. Go under the table.
  c. 10 jumps on the skipping rope.
  d. 10 star jumps 
  e. return back to the start.
3. To make it interesting, time each young person and identify 1st, 2nd and 3rd place for the colony at the end.
4. As each young person finishes, recommend they get a drink. 
5. Tidy and reset for the next group.

## Accessibility and adaptations
Offer paired help, verbal instructions and participation without physical exertion where appropriate.

## Risk controls
* All young people will need to wear shoes.
* Advise young people to watch their heads when going under the table.
* Make sure that the course is kept clear.
* Make sure that water is available to the young people as this will get them warm.
