# Astronaut Fitness Circuit

## Purpose
Explore space-related skills through a short cooperative activity. Suitable for ages 6–8 with adaptations.

## Equipment
4–6 cones or water bottles; floor tape/string; optional timer.

## Setup and instructions
1. Set up a supervised station before the meeting.
2. Explain astronauts exercise to keep muscles and bones strong. Demonstrate a walking zigzag, balancing along a floor line and five star jumps or seated arm raises. Take turns, cheer each other on and reset the route.
3. Tidy and reset for the next group.

## Accessibility and adaptations
Offer paired help, verbal instructions and participation without physical exertion where appropriate.

## Risk controls
No climbing on chairs, pushing or contact races. Provide seated or low-impact options; maintain clear spacing. Leader to review on the day and follow group risk assessment.
