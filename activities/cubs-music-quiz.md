# Purpose and objectives
Identify common instruments and basic musical terms; practise teamwork and respectful listening.

# Equipment
- Screen/projector or large monitor, laptop or phone, optional HDMI adaptor; 1 copy of the [offline presentation](../assets/cubs-music-quiz/slides.html).
- [Mobile-friendly leader answer sheet](../assets/cubs-music-quiz/answers.html).
- Paper and pencils for each team; optional score sheet.
- Low-kit alternative: read questions aloud and score on paper.

# Setup and instructions
1. Test the display and open the slides locally; no internet required.
2. Split Cubs into small teams; explain that each correct answer earns one point.
3. Run all ten question slides without showing answers. Allow about 1 minute per question; teams write A/B/C.
4. Run the answer round, revealing one correct answer at a time. Teams mark their own sheets or swap for marking.
5. Count points out of 10 and congratulate everyone.

# Discussion prompts
What instruments have you tried? What sounds do you hear in your favourite music?

# Accessibility and adaptations
Read each question and option aloud; provide large text on screen; allow additional thinking time and alternative ways to record answers. Avoid making the activity depend on hearing audio clips.
