# Robotic Arm Mission Control

## Purpose
Explore space-related skills through a short cooperative activity. Suitable for ages 6–8 with adaptations.

## Equipment
Kitchen tongs or litter picker; six rolled socks/beanbags; two boxes.

## Setup and instructions
1. Set up a supervised station before the meeting.
2. Explain robotic arms and communication. One Beaver uses tongs to transfer soft objects between boxes while teammates give calm directions. Rotate roles, then try verbal-only instructions. Count group successes.
3. Tidy and reset for the next group.

## Accessibility and adaptations
Offer paired help, verbal instructions and participation without physical exertion where appropriate.

## Risk controls
No blindfolded walking or obstacle courses. Use soft objects, supervised equipment and stationary positions. Leader to review on the day and follow group risk assessment.
