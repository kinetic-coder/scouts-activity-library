# Purpose and objectives
Build interest in space while Cubs practise recalling facts, discussing choices and agreeing an answer as a team. By the end, teams should be able to name several Solar System features and explain at least one surprising space fact.

# Equipment
- Screen/projector or large monitor, laptop or phone, and any required display adaptor.
- [Offline presentation](../assets/cubs-space-quiz/slides.html).
- One [team answer sheet](../assets/cubs-space-quiz/team-sheet.html) and pencil per team.
- [Mobile-friendly leader answers](../assets/cubs-space-quiz/answers.html).
- Low-kit alternative: read the questions and choices aloud, with teams recording A, B or C on scrap paper.

# Setup and instructions
1. Test the display and open the presentation locally. Put seats where every Cub can see without blocking exits.
2. Split the pack into mixed-ability teams of 3–6. Ask each team to use a non-identifying team label rather than personal names.
3. Explain that every question has one answer and each correct answer earns one point. Teams should discuss quietly and make sure everyone can contribute.
4. Show all ten question slides without revealing answers. Allow about one minute for discussion per question.
5. At the marking slide, teams may swap sheets or mark their own. Reveal each answer and briefly share the explanation.
6. Total scores out of 10. Congratulate all teams and invite one surprising fact from each group.

# Discussion prompts
- Which world would you most like to explore, and what equipment would you need?
- Why do astronauts float while orbiting Earth even though gravity is still present?
- Which answer surprised your team most?

# Accessibility and adaptations
- Read every question and option aloud; do not rely only on colour or projected text.
- Seat Cubs who need a clearer view near the front and allow extra processing time.
- Let teams answer by saying, pointing to or holding up A/B/C if writing is a barrier.
- Use mixed-ability groups and rotate the person who records the answer.
- Keep competition friendly; do not ask individuals to answer publicly unless they volunteer.

# Tidy/reset
Collect answer sheets and pencils, switch off and safely store display equipment, remove temporary cable tape and return the room layout.

# Variations
- Make the last question worth two points, announced before it is shown.
- After revealing an answer, award an optional bonus point for a sensible explanation in the team's own words.
- Run as an active corners quiz by labelling three safe areas A, B and C; reassess movement and supervision risks first.
