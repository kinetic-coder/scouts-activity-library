# Purpose and objectives
A friendly team quiz about familiar Marvel characters, designed for Cubs aged 8–10½. Practise listening, collaboration and turn-taking.

# Equipment
- Laptop/projector or mobile device with [offline slides](../assets/cubs-marvel-quiz/slides.html)
- [Mobile-friendly leader answer sheet](../assets/cubs-marvel-quiz/answers.html)
- Paper and pencils for teams; optional scoresheet
- Low-kit alternative: read questions aloud

# Setup and instructions
1. Test the screen or phone and arrange teams of 4–6.
2. Explain one point for each correct answer and respectful participation.
3. Show all 10 question slides first without revealing answers; teams write A/B/C.
4. Then show the same 10 questions in the answer round and reveal each correct answer.
5. Add up scores out of 10, congratulate everyone and invite a favourite-hero discussion.

# Discussion prompts
What makes a superhero helpful? What qualities make a good team?

# Accessibility and adaptations
Read questions aloud; display large text; allow verbal or written answers and extra thinking time. Avoid requiring prior viewing of age-rated films; offer participation without embarrassment for unfamiliarity.
