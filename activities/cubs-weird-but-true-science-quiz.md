# Weird but True Science Quiz

Ten moderately challenging animal-fact questions for Cubs, three choices each.

## Kit
Laptop/projector with [offline slides](../assets/cubs-weird-science-quiz/slides.html), [leader answers](../assets/cubs-weird-science-quiz/answers.html), answer paper and pencils.

## Run
1. Divide into teams of 4–6 and explain A/B/C scoring.
2. Show ten questions without revealing answers.
3. Show the **Stop! Swap Your Answer Sheets!** slide and exchange papers.
4. Reveal answers one at a time, score one point each, and discuss the surprising facts.
5. Collect sheets, tidy and celebrate everyone's contributions.

## Safety and inclusion
Keep projector cables away from walkways. Read questions aloud; support mixed-ability teams; avoid teasing over scores. Leader to complete local risk assessment before use.

## Illustration note
The butterfly answer reveal calls for a humorous butterfly with human-style feet. Current slides use an emoji/text placeholder, **not** a finished illustration.
