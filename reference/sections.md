# UK Scouts section reference

Verified 2026-11-09 from official Scouts sources. **Core age ranges** are not absolute membership boundaries; official Policy, Organisation and Rules (POR) permits specified transitions and reasonable adjustments. Consult POR before making eligibility decisions.

| Section | Core age range | Typical meeting duration (guidance, not a rule) |
|---|---|---|
| Squirrels | 4th to 6th birthday | around 60 minutes |
| Beavers | 6th to 8th birthday | around 60 minutes |
| Cubs | 8th birthday to 10½ years | 60–90 minutes |
| Scouts | 10½ years to 14th birthday | 90–120 minutes |

**Authoritative ages:** https://www.scouts.org.uk/por/4b-our-delivery-sections/4b8-age-ranges/

**Typical meeting durations:** https://www.scouts.org.uk/volunteers/growing-scouts/starting-a-new-section/12-weeks-before-opening/

## Planning checks
- Apply the selected section's core age range to language, attention span, complexity, physical activities and supervision.
- Use local times if known; otherwise do not assume the typical duration is the actual schedule.
- Count opening and closing ceremonies, chant/prayer where locally used, rotations, setup, tidy-up and collection in the timetable.
- Warn when the sum exceeds the window or activities appear too demanding for the age group; suggest specific changes.
- Consult the official current Scouts programme and POR for badge, safety and age rules. Never treat these duration examples as hard limits.
