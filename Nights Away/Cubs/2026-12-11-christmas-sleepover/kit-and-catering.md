# Equipment and catering checklist

## Young people's kit (draft parent/carer guidance)
- Sleeping bag and suitable sleeping mat
- Pyjamas, change of clothes and warm layers
- Toothbrush, toothpaste and wash kit
- Water bottle
- Named bag for belongings and a bag for dirty clothes
- Any required medication handled through the group's approved confidential process

Confirm the final list, bedding needs and venue temperature before distribution.

## Group kit
- Attendance/handover information held securely (not in GitHub)
- First aid kit and appropriate emergency supplies
- Sleeping-area signage and suitable lighting
- Christmas craft materials, table covers, bins and cleaning supplies
- Food preparation and serving equipment, cups and drinking water
- Cleaning materials, bin bags, toilet supplies
- Fire safety and emergency information accessible to leaders

## Food and drink
- Friday evening: light supper and hot chocolate.
- Saturday morning: breakfast.
- Drinking water and other suitable drinks available throughout.
- Agree menu, quantities, food hygiene, allergy/dietary controls and safe hot-drink service once numbers are final.
- Keep individual medical and allergy details in approved private systems, not this repository.

## Procurement and responsibilities
Assign leaders to crafts, shopping, food preparation, washing up, kit checks and venue clean-up after adult staffing is confirmed.
