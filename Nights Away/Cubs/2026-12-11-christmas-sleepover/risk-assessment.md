# Draft risk assessment: Cubs Christmas Sleepover

**Draft only:** event lead must inspect venue and complete risk assessment under current Scouts requirements before use.

| Hazard | Possible harm | Controls to confirm or implement |
|---|---|---|
| Fire / emergency evacuation | Injury, separation | Check exits, alarms, assembly point, roll call, night evacuation plan and sleeping layout; brief everyone |
| Mixed-gender sleeping / changing | Privacy or safeguarding concern | Agree separate/private arrangements and appropriate adult supervision before event |
| Multiple rooms / buildings | Unsupervised movement | Adult cover in each occupied area; controlled transitions and clear boundaries |
| Toilets / washing | Privacy and safeguarding | Agree access, adult positioning, privacy and welfare assistance procedures |
| Hot chocolate and hot food | Scalds, burns | Adult preparation/service, appropriate temperatures and safe equipment |
| Food allergies and hygiene | Allergic reaction or illness | Securely check dietary needs, prevent cross-contact, apply food hygiene controls |
| Crafts and equipment | Cuts, trips, inappropriate material use | Age-appropriate materials, supervised tools, tidy workspaces and cleaning |
| Sleeping areas / bags | Trips, blocked exits | Clear walkways, safe spacing, no obstruction of exits or emergency equipment |
| Illness, anxiety or homesickness | Distress or medical issue | Welfare lead, quiet space, confidential care plans, parent/carer contact route |
| Overnight fatigue / insufficient cover | Reduced supervision | Agreed adult rota, backup cover and escalation plan |
| Arrival and collection | Wrong handover or missing child | Secure register and authorised collection procedure outside GitHub |

## Before sign-off
- Confirm actual room permissions, occupancy limits and sleeping configuration.
- Review accessibility, individual support needs and relevant venue hazards privately.
- Record event lead review, approval and any amendments in the group's approved process.
- Check current Scouts Nights Away, safeguarding and activity guidance; this draft does not replace it.
